What are we playing?

Different moods call for different games. Use this genre guide as a starting point for your next download or weekend marathon.

COMPETITIVE

Action & shooters

Fast reflexes, close calls, and the satisfying feeling of a perfectly timed play. Great when you want high-energy sessions.

CO-OP

Party & multiplayer

Bring friends, embrace the chaos, and remember: a little friendly competition is still friendship. Mostly.

IMMERSIVE

Adventure & RPGs

Explore strange places, meet memorable characters, and get distracted by side quests for six hours.

CREATIVE

Sandbox & building

Make a home, a city, or an architectural crime against nature. The only limit is your imagination and available storage.

THINKING CAP

Puzzles & strategy

For players who love clever systems, tough decisions, and the moment a complicated plan finally clicks.

LOW PRESSURE

Cozy & casual

Relax, collect things, decorate spaces, and enjoy a game that doesn't treat every mistake like a season finale.
