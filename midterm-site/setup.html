Comfort is an upgrade

Build a setup that works for you.

A great gaming space isn't about buying every gadget. Start with comfort, good habits, and a few thoughtful improvements.

Start with the essentials

Before chasing RGB lights and the newest hardware, make sure your space supports the way you actually play. Small improvements can make long sessions more comfortable and your desk easier to use.

  • 01 · ErgonomicsKeep your screen at a comfortable height and relax your shoulders.
  • 02 · LightingUse soft ambient light to reduce harsh contrast with the screen.
  • 03 · AudioChoose headphones or speakers that suit your room and play style.
  • 04 · Cable controlGroup cables and leave enough slack for moving your devices safely.
Upgrade in order

Smart setup checklist

FIRST

Comfort

Chair, desk height, posture breaks, and a screen position that doesn't make you crane your neck.

NEXT

Reliability

Stable internet, updated drivers, sensible backups, and enough storage for the games you play.

THEN

Personality

Add lighting, art, accessories, and decorations that make the space feel like yours.
