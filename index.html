About SUNY New Paltz

State University of New York at New Paltz is a public university located in New Paltz, New York. The campus combines a strong liberal arts tradition with professional programs, creative opportunities, and a close-knit campus community.

Academics

Liberal Arts

Explore subjects across the humanities, social sciences, natural sciences, and interdisciplinary studies.

Business & Professional Studies

Develop practical skills and knowledge through programs designed to prepare students for professional careers.

Arts & Design

Discover opportunities in visual arts, design, music, theatre, and other creative disciplines.
