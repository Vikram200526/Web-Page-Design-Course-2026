Academics

Explore challenging programs, discover your interests, and prepare for your future at SUNY New Paltz.

Learn. Create. Discover.

SUNY New Paltz offers students a broad academic experience with opportunities to study the arts, sciences, business, education, technology, and many other fields. Students can develop their skills while working closely with faculty and becoming active members of their academic communities.

Areas of Study

🎨 Arts & Design

Explore creativity through art, design, music, theatre, and other visual and performing arts disciplines.

🔬 Science & Engineering

Study the natural world through science, technology, mathematics, engineering, and hands-on learning experiences.

📚 Humanities

Develop critical thinking and communication skills through literature, history, philosophy, languages, and culture.

💼 Business

Build knowledge in areas such as management, marketing, finance, entrepreneurship, and economics.

👩‍🏫 Education

Prepare for careers in education with programs focused on teaching, learning, leadership, and child development.

🌎 Social Sciences

Examine people, communities, and society through disciplines such as psychology, sociology, political science, and more.
